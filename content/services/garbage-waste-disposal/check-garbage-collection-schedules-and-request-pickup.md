# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — City of Caloocan

The City Government of Caloocan manages **regular garbage collection** through the **City Environmental Management Department (CEMD)** and barangays. Residents and business owners are encouraged to follow the schedule and proper segregation rules.

---

## 1) Regular Garbage Collection

**How it works**

- Collection schedules vary **by barangay** — Caloocan has 188 barangays, and truck routes/days are set at the barangay level rather than one citywide schedule.
- Trucks assigned to barangays cover **household waste**, **biodegradable**, and **residual/non-recyclable** garbage.
- Segregation at source is **mandatory** under Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act) — separate biodegradable, recyclable, and residual/special waste.

**Where to check schedules**

1. **Barangay Hall** — each barangay posts its official collection schedule; this is the most reliable source since schedules are set per barangay, not citywide.
2. **Barangay Facebook Pages** — most update residents when trucks are delayed or rescheduled.
3. **City Environmental Management Department (CEMD)** — for citywide advisories (e.g., during holidays or typhoon disruptions).

> Tip: Post the collection calendar on your fridge or gate to remind the household which days to take bins out.

---

## 2) Special Garbage Pickup (Bulk or Non-Routine Waste)

For **bulk waste** (e.g., furniture, appliances, construction debris, yard trimmings), collection is not part of the regular schedule.

**How to request**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** or contact the barangay's environment/sanitation desk.
2. Provide details: type of waste, estimated volume, and address.
3. The barangay coordinates with **CEMD** for scheduling a **special truck pickup**.
4. Some barangays may require a **minimal fee** for bulk or special disposal — confirm with your barangay.

**Items usually needing special pickup**

- Large furniture, mattresses, appliances, e-waste
- Construction/demolition debris
- Hazardous household waste (paints, chemicals, batteries)

## 4) Quick Tips for Residents

- Put garbage out **only on collection day**, early morning or the night before.
- Use durable, closed containers to prevent animal scattering.
- For recyclables, check if your barangay has a **Materials Recovery Facility (MRF)** or partner junkshop — RA 9003 requires an MRF per barangay or cluster of barangays, though space constraints mean not every barangay in Caloocan has operationalized one yet.
- During **rainy season/typhoons**, expect **schedule adjustments** — follow barangay or CEMD announcements.
- Avoid burning waste (strictly prohibited under RA 9003 and the Clean Air Act).

---

## 5) Businesses & Establishments

- Businesses must comply with **segregation** and may need to contract **accredited private haulers** for bulk/industrial waste.
- Keep proof of disposal (receipts/hauling contracts) for compliance inspections.

---

### Key Reminder

Following your barangay's **garbage collection schedule** and properly requesting **special pickups** keeps the city clean and prevents flooding, pests, and health risks.
