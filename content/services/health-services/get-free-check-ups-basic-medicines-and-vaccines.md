# City Government of Caloocan: Free Health Services

The City Government of Caloocan offers free **check-ups, medicines, and vaccines** through the City Health Department (CHD), barangay health centers, and the city's two government hospitals — Caloocan City Medical Center and Caloocan City North Medical Center.

---

## 1. Free Medical Check-ups

| Service              | Where                        | Schedule                                     | Requirements                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| General consultation | City/Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM (varies by health center) | Individual Treatment Record (ITR) — issued on first visit |

---

## 2. Free Basic Medicines

| Service                | Where                                 | Schedule           | Requirements                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free basic medicines   | City Health Department pharmacy       | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM | Doctor's prescription and/or referral from hospital/clinic |
| Prescription medicines | Caloocan City Medical Center pharmacy | Hospital hours     | Doctor's prescription from a CCMC physician                |

---

## 3. Free Vaccines & Immunization

| Beneficiary       | Vaccine/Service                                          | Where                                                                                                         | Schedule                                    | Requirements                                |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Children          | BCG, Pentavalent, OPV/IPV, MMR, Hepatitis B, PCV13 (EPI) | Barangay Health Centers                                                                                       | Typically Wednesdays, 8 AM–4 PM             | ECCD card (first-timers can get one onsite) |
| Pregnant women    | Tetanus Toxoid vaccine + supplements                     | Barangay Health Centers                                                                                       | During prenatal services                    | Proof of pregnancy, ECCD/record             |
| Animal bite cases | Anti-rabies vaccine (free)                               | Any of the city's 8 Animal Bite Treatment Centers, or the 24-hour center at Caloocan City Medical Center's ER | Mon–Fri, 1 PM–5 PM (ABTCs); 24/7 at CCMC ER | Proof of Caloocan residency                 |

---

## How to Avail

1. **Check-ups** → Go to your Barangay Health Center or the City Health Department; first-timers will be issued an ITR.
2. **Medicines** → Bring a doctor's prescription/referral; submit at the City Health Department pharmacy window, or the CCMC pharmacy if prescribed there.
3. **Child immunization** → Visit your barangay health center on immunization day with your ECCD card.
4. **Pregnancy services** → Attend prenatal visits at the barangay health center for free vaccine and supplements.
5. **Animal bite / anti-rabies** → Report to the nearest Animal Bite Treatment Center (Mon–Fri, 1 PM–5 PM) or, for after-hours emergencies, the 24-hour center at Caloocan City Medical Center's Emergency Room; bring proof of residency.
