# Transparency Documents

The City Government of Caloocan publishes a Transparency Seal on its official website, in compliance with Section 91 (Transparency Seal) of Republic Act No. 10633 (General Appropriations Act, FY 2014), covering the following categories:

---

## Available Documents & Portals

- **Full Disclosure Policy Portal** — Budget and financial disclosure documents required under the DILG's Full Disclosure Policy.
- **Citizen's Charter** — Step-by-step service standards for frontline government transactions.
- **Executive Orders** — Issuances from the Office of the City Mayor.
- **Civil Society Organization (CSO) accreditation** — Records of accredited CSOs participating in local governance.
- **National Privacy Commission Certificate of Registration** — The city's data privacy compliance registration.
- **Local Climate Change Action Plan** (2017–2025)
- **Local Shelter Plan** (2017–2025)
- **Local Development Investment Plan** (2020–2022)
- **Comprehensive Land Use Plan** (2019)
- **Comprehensive Development Plan** (2023–2028)
- **Ecological Profile** (2025)
- **Executive-Legislative Agenda** (2023–2025)
- **Procurement** — Bids and awards records.

---

## Where to Find Them

These documents are published on the City Government of Caloocan's official Transparency page. This portal links to it directly rather than mirroring the files, since they are updated periodically by the city government.

**Official source:** [caloocancity.gov.ph/transparency-seal](https://caloocancity.gov.ph/transparency-seal/)

## Other Ways to Request Government Information
