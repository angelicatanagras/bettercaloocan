# Statistics

Population and demographic data for the City of Caloocan, as recorded by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).

---

## Population

| Census Year | Population | Change from Previous Census |
| ----------- | ---------- | --------------------------- |
| 2024        | 1,712,945  | +3.09% (from 2020)          |
| 2020        | 1,661,584  | —                           |
| 2010        | 1,489,040  | —                           |

Caloocan is a highly urbanized city in the National Capital Region (Metro Manila), administratively divided across three congressional districts.

---

## Notes

- Figures above are official counts from the PSA's Census of Population (2024, 2020, and 2010 rounds), not estimates or projections.
- For the most current official statistics, visit the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://psa.gov.ph).
