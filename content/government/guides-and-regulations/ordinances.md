# Ordinances & Resolutions

Caloocan's local laws are enacted by the **Sangguniang Panlungsod** (City Council), the city's 24-member legislative body, presided over by the Vice Mayor. Ordinances carry the force of law within the city; resolutions express the Council's position or authorize specific administrative actions.

---

## How an Ordinance Becomes Law

Under the Local Government Code of 1991, a proposed ordinance must pass **three readings** on separate days before the full Council, be signed by the presiding officer (the Vice Mayor), and be approved or allowed to lapse into law by the City Mayor. The FY2025 Annual Budget Ordinance, for example, went through this same three-reading process before being transmitted to the Mayor on October 17, 2024 (see [Budget & City Finances](/government/reports-and-statistics/budget)).

## Where to Find the Full Text

The City Government does not currently maintain a public, searchable online database of ordinances — the official site's ordinances page (`caloocancity.gov.ph/ufaq/ordinances/`) returns a "page not found" error as of this writing, and the Transparency page's Executive Orders / CSO section links are non-functional placeholders rather than live documents.

Until that's fixed, the most reliable ways to get the full text of a specific ordinance or resolution are:

- **Sangguniang Panlungsod ng Caloocan (official Facebook page)** — regularly posts session summaries, resolutions, and budget-related actions: [facebook.com/caloocancitycouncil](https://www.facebook.com/caloocancitycouncil)
- **Request directly** from the Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod, via the [official directory](https://caloocancity.gov.ph/about-us/directory/)
- **Freedom of Information (FOI) request** at [foi.gov.ph](https://www.foi.gov.ph)
